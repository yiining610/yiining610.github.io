2026 ASLA Student Awards · Honor Award, Research

When Heat Does Not Stop Walking? Landscape Adaptation for Older Adults

In Harris County, older adults near public housing continued walking under high heat stress and cut back only as conditions approached dangerous thresholds. The project translates this finding into design strategies such as shaded routes, cooling corridors, and places to rest and cool down.
